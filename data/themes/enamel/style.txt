A desktop icon in the BeOS tradition, refined for a modern high-resolution display. Every icon is made as a hard enamel pin whose artwork is the BeOS drawing of the object: every outline and dividing line is a thick raised line of polished gold-toned metal, like cloisonné, and the areas between are filled with smooth, flat, slightly glossy coloured enamel, one colour per face, so the lighter top face and the darker side faces are different enamels. The pin's metal edge follows the silhouette. Palette: Be blue, BeOS tab yellow, signal red, leaf green, white and warm greys. A soft semi-transparent cast shadow to the lower right, as if the pin lay on the desk. No photographic texture. The object is drawn as a BeOS icon: in a strong three-quarter view from above and to the left, sitting on an imaginary desk that recedes towards the upper right, so its top face is clearly visible; flat things such as paper, envelopes and folders lie on the desk at an angle, never facing the viewer. Chunky, simple, friendly proportions with little fine detail, so the silhouette reads at 16 pixels. Light from the upper left. No text, no letters, no watermark. Centred on a fully transparent background with generous padding; nothing else in the frame.
