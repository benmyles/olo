A desktop icon drawn the way NeXTSTEP drew its icons, refined for a modern high-resolution display. Precise 1990s workstation icon art with the weight of a photograph: mostly the four greys of a NeXT display (black, dark grey, light grey and white) with restrained colour in deep, slightly muted tones (a deep blue, a deep yellow, brick red, a little green), crisp dark outlines, soft airbrushed shading and fine highlights like a careful technical illustration, real materials (brushed metal, matte black plastic, paper). Seen as NeXTSTEP showed things: nearly straight on, at eye level, a little perspective only where it gives the object its depth (a box or a cube seen slightly from above), never laid flat on a desk.
