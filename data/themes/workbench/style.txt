A desktop icon drawn the way AmigaOS 3's Workbench drew its icons in the MagicWB style, refined for a modern high-resolution display while keeping its pixels. Crisp pixel art on a coarse, clearly visible pixel grid with no anti-aliasing: a small fixed palette of light grey, mid grey, dark grey, black, white, steel blue, a pale sky blue and a warm beige, ordered dithering for shading, hard black outlines one pixel wide and bright one-pixel highlights on the lit edges, like a 1990s Amiga screen at a large zoom. Seen as Workbench showed things: front on and flat, like a sprite, with only a little perspective for boxes and machines; a folder is a Workbench drawer, the front of a grey filing drawer with a handle; a document is an upright page with a turned corner, the thing it holds drawn on it. No cast shadow on a desk, only the outline's dark lower-right edge. Chunky, simple shapes, so the silhouette reads at 16 pixels. Light from the upper left. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermark. Centred on a fully transparent background with generous padding; nothing else in the frame.
